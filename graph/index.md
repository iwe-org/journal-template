# 📔 Journal

Your journal and the people in it. Write about your days in [Journal](journal.md), keep a page for everyone who matters in [People](people.md), and record what happens in their lives in [Events](events.md). Every person you mention in an entry or an event is linked, so a person's page shows, through its back-links, every day you saw them and everything that happened to them.

## Start here

1. Open [Anna Novak](people/anna-novak.md) to see a person's page: who she is, how she is related to the others, her pets, a gift idea and the tent you lent her. Its back-links list the entries and events that mention her.
2. Open [2026-11-08](journal/2026-11-08.md) to see a journal entry that links the people in it.
3. Ask your agent to write today's entry from a few lines you tell it. It links the people you mention, adds anyone new, and notes when you last saw them.
4. Ask your agent who has a birthday coming up, or who you have not been in touch with for a while.

The documents above are examples. When you are ready, ask your agent to remove the examples; it deletes every document marked `example: true` and leaves the hubs.
